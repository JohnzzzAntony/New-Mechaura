Elevator Accessories & Spares: Essential Inspection & Safety Checklist in Dubai

August 2026 6 min read Mechaura Technical Desk
In short

Lift wear parts follow predictable replacement cycles: guide shoe inserts every 12–24 months, door rollers on condition, governor and safety gear on annual test. Door faults cause the majority of entrapment call-outs, so door-system components deserve the closest inspection attention.

What counts as an elevator wear part

Elevator accessories are the consumable and safety-critical components of a lift installation: the guide rails and shoes that locate the car in the shaft, the door operator and its rollers, the overspeed governor and safety gear, and the buffers in the pit.

They are not permanent fixtures. Each has a defined inspection interval and a replacement cycle, and treating them as run-to-failure items is the root cause of most unplanned lift downtime in commercial buildings.

The quarterly inspection checklist

  1. Guide shoe liners. Check for lateral play in the car. Excess play means the liner has worn and the car is beginning to rock — replace before it damages the rail face.
  2. Door rollers. Look for flat spots, radial play and audible noise on operation. Door faults account for the largest share of entrapment call-outs.
  3. Door skates and locks. Verify engagement depth and that the lock circuit breaks correctly. Any lock fault is an immediate out-of-service condition.
  4. Governor switch. Confirm operation and that the trip speed setting matches the rated speed on the installation certificate.
  5. Buffers. Inspect polyurethane buffers for compression set and cracking; check oil level on hydraulic buffers.
  6. Traction ropes. Check for broken wires, diameter reduction and uneven tension between ropes.

High-traffic residential towers and buildings with heavy goods-lift use sit at the short end of every range in that table. Low-rise offices with light duty sit at the long end.

Compliance in the UAE

Components supplied for UAE installations should be manufactured to EN 81-20 and EN 81-50, the standards referenced by UAE Civil Defence and municipality lift requirements. For safety-critical parts — safety gear, governors, locks — declarations of conformity and test certificates should be obtained and retained with the maintenance record.

When a lift is inspected and a component cannot be evidenced as conforming, the practical outcome is the same as if it were non-conforming. Keep the certificates with the log book, not in a supplier email.

Sourcing spares for older installations

The hardest part of lift maintenance in a mature building stock is obsolete spares. Original manufacturers discontinue parts, and building managers are told the only option is full modernisation.

That is often not true. Most wear parts are dimensionally standard even when the part number is dead. Send the make, model and a photograph or the worn component, and a dimensional equivalent can usually be identified. See our elevator accessories and spares range.

Common questions

How frequently should elevator guide shoes and door rollers be replaced?

Guide shoe inserts should be inspected quarterly and replaced every 12 to 24 months depending on traffic intensity. Door rollers are replaced on condition — immediately if radial play, flat spots or noise develop.

What is the difference between progressive and instantaneous safety gear?

Instantaneous gear grips the rail abruptly and is only permitted on lower-speed lifts. Progressive gear brakes over a controlled distance to limit deceleration, and is required above roughly 1 m/s rated speed. The lift design determines which is fitted.

Can spares be supplied for a discontinued lift model?

Usually yes. Most wear parts are dimensionally standard even when the original part number is obsolete. Provide the make, model and a photograph or sample and a dimensional equivalent can generally be sourced.

Are EN 81-20 components accepted in the UAE?

Yes. EN 81-20 and EN 81-50 are the component standards referenced by UAE Civil Defence and municipality lift requirements. Conformity declarations should be retained with the maintenance record.
